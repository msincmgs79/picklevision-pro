TROUBLESHOOTING
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

A: Clear browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+Delete) and reload

Q: Stats still show 0%?
A: Hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R). Verify new analyses were created.

Q: Opponent names don't show?
A: Check Firestore has "opponent" field in recent documents.

Q: Vercel deployment stuck?
A: Go to Vercel dashboard and click "Redeploy"
